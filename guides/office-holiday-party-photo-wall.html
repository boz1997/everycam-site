Office Holiday Party Photo Wall: Guest Photos on the TV, No App

Updated November 2026 · 7-minute read

The office holiday party is often the one night most of the company is in the same room, and its photos usually stay scattered across everyone’s phones. A live photo wall puts them on the big screen while the party is still going: coworkers scan the code on their table, upload from the phone’s browser, and their photos fade in on the TV as they arrive.

Here is how the ShareCam live wall works, what you need, how to set it up before the party, and what the host controls on the night.

How the live wall works

The wall is a web page. Open sharecam.app/wall on any TV, laptop or projector: no cable to anyone’s phone, no app. New photos fade in as they arrive, with your event name, code and join QR always on screen, so the wall also tells people how to join.

  • Photos only. The wall skips videos; they stay in the album.
  • Open albums only. The wall is for open albums. If you use private mode, where guests see only their own photos, start the wall after you reveal the album.
  • Included with Unlimited. Among the event packages, the wall comes with Unlimited ($39.99, one-time, per event). There is no separate fee.

What you need

  • A screen with a browser: a smart TV with a browser, a laptop plugged into the TV or projector, or a Chromecast or AirPlay target you can cast a browser tab to.
  • Internet at the screen. The wall streams photos, so it needs Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. At the office, check the guest Wi-Fi a week before; at an outside venue, ask the venue.
  • Table cards. ShareCam has 16 card designs with your QR code built in. Print four to an A4 page with cut marks, or send the invite link.

How coworkers join from the browser

The QR code opens in the phone’s browser, on iPhone or Android. Guests type their name, pick photos, videos or a whole folder, and they’re in. No app, no account, no password.

  • Lost the card? The 6-character code works too, at sharecam.app/join.
  • A name on every photo. Every upload carries the guest’s name, and the host sees the full guest list.
  • Light on the Wi-Fi. Photos are resized on the phone to 2,048 px before upload, so they go up quickly. On Unlimited, the full-resolution original is kept as well.
  • App or browser, same album. Anyone who prefers the app can use it; everything lands in one place.

Setting it up before the party

  1. Create the event in the ShareCam iPhone app or on the web dashboard: name, date, and open rather than private. Your QR code is ready in about a minute.
  2. Choose Unlimited. Check the event name and date first: they lock once you choose a paid package.
  3. Print the cards. Put one on every table, one at the bar and one next to the screen. People who see the wall look for the code.
  4. Start the wall. On the screen’s browser, open sharecam.app/wall and enter the event code, or open the wall link from the event in the app (Event → Live wall). Go full screen and turn off the screen’s sleep timer; on a laptop, plug it in and disable sleep for the evening.
  5. Test it. Upload one photo from your own phone before people arrive. If it shows up on the wall, you are done.
  6. Say it once. One sentence in the welcome toast or the invite email (“scan the code on your table and your photos go up on the screen”) is usually all the explanation people need.

What the host controls on the night

In an open album the wall shows photos as they arrive, so the host’s controls work after a photo is up, not before. Decide in advance who keeps the host’s app or dashboard open during the party.

  • Hide or delete any photo. A hidden photo leaves the wall within seconds.
  • Reports go to the host. If a guest reports a photo, it disappears for them at once and waits in the host’s Reported list.
  • Remove a guest or pause new joins. The wall shows the join QR and code the whole time, so anyone who can see the screen can join. Pause new joins when the party winds down. Removing a guest frees their slot.
  • Downloads. You decide whether guests can save photos too.

A few things to settle at work

  • Tell people about the screen. Mention in the invite that guest photos will appear on a screen at the party. It is a courtesy, and it heads off awkward moments.
  • Who can see the album. Albums are reachable only through the event’s QR code or 6-character invite code, and in an open album every guest who joins sees the shared album.
  • Using the photos later. Our terms give the host permission to view and download guests’ uploads as part of the album; further use, such as posting someone’s photo publicly, is between the people at the event. Ask before a photo goes on the company’s social accounts.
  • Removal requests. Someone who appears in a photo and doesn’t use ShareCam can ask for its removal by writing to app.sharecam@gmail.com with the event code; no account is needed.
  • Where the photos are stored. Albums are stored in the EU: the database in Frankfurt, photos and videos in Belgium. We do not sell or rent your data, we do not show ads inside the app, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • Receipts. On the web, Paddle runs the checkout, sends the receipt and invoice and handles tax. Purchases in the iPhone app are processed by Apple.
  • Face matching is separate. It is an optional add-on that lets each guest find their own photos with a selfie. It is off until the host turns it on, every guest decides for themselves, and our terms do not allow it to be used to track attendance or to monitor employees. See how face matching works.

What it costs

Pay once, per event: no subscription, nothing renews. Every event starts free on Spark. These are the event packages as listed in the pricing section of our home page:

  • Spark, free: 10 guests, 50 photos, no videos, 7 days of storage.
  • Party, $14.99: 50 guests, 200 photos, 5 videos, 1 month of storage.
  • Wedding, $24.99: 100 guests, 500 photos, 20 videos, 6 months of storage.
  • Unlimited, $39.99: unlimited guests, photos and videos, originals kept, 1 year of storage. For more than 100 guests, and the package that includes the live wall.

Face matching is a +$9.99 add-on to any package in the iPhone app, where available.

The live wall is included with Unlimited (open albums, photos only), so a team of 40 that wants the wall still needs Unlimited. Prices are in USD. In the app, the App Store charges in your local currency; on the web, Paddle shows local currency and tax at checkout. Storage is counted from your event date, not the day you create it.

If you hire a photographer instead, the photographer packages work differently: the photographer uploads from a computer, and guests view and find their photos with a selfie. Pro 1000 ($89.99) and up include the wall.

After the party

Save the whole album to Photos or download it as a ZIP. On Unlimited, the full-resolution originals are kept as well, and the host downloads them from a computer. Unlimited keeps the album for a year from the event date, and you’re always warned before storage expires: photos are never silently deleted.

Questions

Do coworkers need to install an app?

No. The QR code opens in the phone’s browser, on iPhone or Android. They type their name and upload. Anyone who prefers the app can use it, and everything lands in the same album.

Which package includes the live wall?

Unlimited ($39.99, one-time, per event), in open albums. The photographer packages from Pro 1000 up include it too. There is no separate fee.

Can we take a photo off the wall?

Yes. The host can hide or delete any photo, and a hidden photo leaves the wall within seconds. Guests can report a photo, and it goes straight to the host.

Does the wall play videos?

No. The wall shows photos only and skips videos. Videos stay in the album.
