The disposable camera alternative for weddings

Also in Deutsch · Español · Français

Updated October 2026 · 5-minute read

Disposable cameras on wedding tables are back, and for a good reason: nobody knows what is on them until the film comes back, and that surprise is lovely. The rest of the experience is less lovely. Cameras get lost, half the frames are of the ceiling, developing and scanning cost real money, and the photos arrive two weeks after the honeymoon in a resolution that will not print larger than a postcard.

Keep the surprise, lose the film

The part people love about disposables is the reveal. A QR photo album can give you exactly that. In ShareCam’s private mode, guests upload from their own phones but never see anyone else’s photos. The couple sees everything as it arrives, or waits and opens the album after the wedding, by hand or at a scheduled moment. From the guest’s side it feels like dropping a roll of film in a box; from the couple’s side it is one tidy album with a name on every photo.

What changes in practice

  • Cost. One package per event instead of one camera per table plus developing. The first ShareCam event is free; wedding packages are a one-time purchase.
  • Quality. Modern phone photos instead of 27 frames of ISO 400 film under reception lighting. On the Unlimited package the full-resolution originals are kept too.
  • Video. Disposables cannot do the first dance. Guests can add videos of up to two minutes on Party and above.
  • Zero logistics. No collecting cameras at the end of the night, no lab, no scanning. The album is the delivery.
  • Names. Every upload carries the guest’s name, which turns “who took this?” into a non-question.

What you give up

Honesty helps here. You lose the physical object on the table and the film look. If the aesthetic is the point, keep a few disposables for the vibe and use the QR album as the real collection channel. Many couples do exactly that: cameras as decoration, album as the archive.

How the reveal works

  1. Create the event in private mode and print the table cards (16 designs ship with the app, four per A4 sheet).
  2. During the wedding, guests scan and upload. They see only their own uploads.
  3. After the wedding, open the album. It becomes a shared gallery for everyone with the code, or stays yours alone if you prefer.

A note on guests who don’t do technology

Scanning a QR code is the camera app now, on both iPhone and Android, and the 6-character code typed at sharecam.app/join is the fallback for everyone else. No app store visit, no account, no password. In our experience the person who “doesn’t do apps” uploads more photos than anyone once they realise it is just the browser.

Try it on your own event. Create a free event in the ShareCam iPhone app or on the web dashboard, print the QR card, and see the album fill up. Paid packages are one-time, per event.
