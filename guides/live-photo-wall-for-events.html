A live photo wall for your venue, set up in five minutes

Updated October 2026 · 4-minute read

A live photo wall is the single best upload motivator we know. Guests upload a photo, it appears on the big screen thirty seconds later, the table cheers, three more people take out their phones. It also happens to be beautiful: a slow rotation of the evening, as it is happening, from a hundred angles.

What it is

A full-screen slideshow of the event album that refreshes itself as new photos arrive. With ShareCam it is a web page, sharecam.app/wall, opened with your event code. Nothing to install on the venue’s equipment and no phone held hostage by an HDMI cable all night.

What you need

  • A screen: a smart TV with a browser, a laptop plugged into the projector, or a Chromecast / AirPlay target you can cast a browser tab to.
  • Internet at the venue: the wall streams photos, so it needs Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. Ask the venue a week before; this is the thing that goes wrong.
  • The wall link: in the ShareCam app, Event → Live wall. The link carries the code; keep the browser tab open.

Setting it up

  1. Open the wall link on the screen’s browser and press the full-screen button.
  2. Turn off the screen’s sleep timer. On a laptop, plug it in and disable sleep for the evening.
  3. Test with one photo from your own phone before guests arrive. If it shows up on the wall, you are done.
  4. Put a table card next to the screen too. People who see the wall look for the code.

Good practice

  • Moderation stays with the host. Hide a photo in the app and it leaves the wall within seconds.
  • Private mode and the wall. The wall is for open albums. If you run private mode for the surprise, switch the wall on after the reveal, for the party part of the night.
  • Speed matters more than resolution. Guest photos are resized on the phone before upload exactly so the wall keeps up on venue Wi-Fi.

For photographers

If you shoot the event and upload from a laptop during the night, the wall is the fastest way to deliver a preview: guests see your frames on the screen within a minute of upload, and find themselves in the gallery with a selfie afterwards. The photographer packages from Pro 1000 up include the wall.

Try it on your own event. Create a free event in the ShareCam iPhone app or on the web dashboard, print the QR card, and see the album fill up. Paid packages are one-time, per event.
