Find Every Photo You’re In at a Wedding, With One Selfie

Updated November 2026 · 7-minute read

At a wedding, every guest’s phone fills one live album, often with thousands of photos. Somewhere in there are the photos you’re in, including the ones you’d never have seen: the toast from the back row, the dance floor at 1 a.m.

ShareCam’s face matching is the shortcut. Each guest takes a selfie in the browser and sees only the photos they appear in, ready to save. No app, no scrolling through thousands. It is optional, it is off until the host turns it on, and every guest decides for themselves.

How it works

  1. The host turns it on. Face matching is off by default for every event. The host adds it in the ShareCam iPhone app, where available, and accepts the host terms before the one-time purchase. Photographer packages include it.
  2. Guests are told. Turning it on includes a promise to tell guests. The app gives the host wording for the invitation and a line for the table card, and guests who join in a web browser see a notice on the join screen before they upload anything.
  3. A guest opts in. No selfie is taken or compared until the guest reads the consent screen and taps to agree. There is no pre-ticked box, and guests are also asked to confirm where they are.
  4. One selfie. The guest takes a selfie in the browser. ShareCam turns it into a face signature, a mathematical description of the geometry of a face, and compares it with the faces in the photos of that one event’s album.
  5. The matches. The guest sees only the photos they appear in, ready to save to Photos or download as a ZIP, as long as the host allows guest downloads.

If nothing turns up yet, more photos may still be uploading. Try again later in the evening or the next day.

What happens to your selfie

  • It is processed on our servers, not on your phone. The face-matching engine is Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany. It acts as our processor and may not use face data for its own purposes.
  • It is deleted quickly. The search signature is deleted as soon as the search finishes, within 24 hours at the latest, and the selfie is never added to the album.
  • It searches one album only. A search covers only the photos uploaded to that one event’s album: never the web, social media or any other public or leaked source. Nothing is generated: no face swaps, no deepfakes.
  • It is never reused. Face data is never sold, never used to train AI, and never used to identify anyone across events.
  • Nobody gets a list of people. Signatures are never shown to anyone, and the host cannot look a guest up. Even the host searches with their own selfie.

The faces in the album, and why the host tells guests

To make the album searchable, ShareCam creates a signature for each face in the album’s photos once the host turns the feature on. That includes people who never opened ShareCam and never agreed to anything. Our notice calls this a real limitation of any feature of this kind, and it is the reason for these rules:

  • The feature is off by default for every event, and a host has to switch it on.
  • Turning it on requires the host to confirm that they will tell their guests, with wording for the invitation and for a sign at the event.
  • Anyone who appears in an album can write to app.sharecam@gmail.com and have their signatures deleted, whether or not they use ShareCam.

Album signatures are destroyed when the album is deleted, which happens automatically when the event’s storage period ends (7 days to 1 year, depending on the package), or immediately if the host deletes the event. If the host switches the feature off, every album signature for that event is destroyed.

Saying no costs nothing

  • Each guest decides for themselves. Declining costs nothing and never limits access to the album: guests can still join, browse and download as before.
  • You can change your mind. Withdraw at any time, from the same screen or by writing to us. Withdrawal deletes your signatures and removes you from any matches.
  • You can ask what we hold. Ask for a copy of your face data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, at app.sharecam@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, and there is no charge.

Where it isn’t offered

Face matching is not offered in Illinois, Texas or Washington, in China, Russia or Vietnam, or anywhere we can’t confirm the location. Those three states have dedicated biometric privacy laws, and we would rather withhold a convenience feature than operate in a gray area.

The location check is based on the internet connection, so it can be wrong behind a VPN; that is why guests are also asked to confirm where they are. Hosts confirm that their event is not taking place in Illinois, Texas or Washington. If your wedding is in one of those states, everything else in the album works without face matching.

When a photographer shoots the wedding

With a photographer package, the photographer uploads the whole gallery from a computer, and originals are kept byte for byte. Guests scan the table QR and find the photos they’re in with a selfie, then browse the rest: no app, unlimited guests, face matching included where available. The couple gets a one-time code for the album page and downloads everything as ZIP parts, in 2,048 px or originals.

Tips for the day

  • Use both notices. Put the face matching wording in the invitation and the line on the table card; the host terms ask for both.
  • Good light helps. Look straight at the camera in decent light. You can retake the selfie as many times as you like.
  • Search again later. Guests keep uploading during the evening and after it, so a second search the next day can find more.
  • Save before storage ends. Storage is counted from the event date, and the host is always warned before it expires.

Questions

Do guests need an app to find their photos?

No. Each guest takes a selfie in the browser and sees only the photos they appear in.

Can the host see who is in which photo?

No. Face matching never tells a host who appears in which photo: there is no list of people, and signatures are never shown to anyone. Even the host searches with their own selfie.

How long is the selfie kept?

The search signature is deleted as soon as the search finishes, within 24 hours at the latest, and the selfie is never added to the album.

What does face matching cost?

$9.99 once per event on any event package, added in the iPhone app where available. It is included in the photographer packages.
