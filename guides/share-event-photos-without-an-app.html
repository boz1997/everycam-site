How to collect event photos from guests without an app

Updated October 2026 · 5-minute read

The moment you ask guests to install something, you lose half of them. The rest install it, forget it, and send you their photos by message weeks later, compressed to oblivion. Here is what actually works for collecting photos at a wedding, birthday or company event, ranked by how much it asks of the guest.

1. A shared QR album (asks: scan, type a name)

One QR code on each table opens an upload page in the phone’s browser. Guests type a name, pick photos and videos, done. Everything lands in one album that the host owns, with the guest’s name on each upload. This is what ShareCam does at sharecam.app/join; the host runs the event from the iPhone app or the web dashboard, guests never install anything. It also works after the event, for as long as the album is kept.

2. A cloud folder link (asks: sign in, find the upload button)

A shared Drive or iCloud folder is free and familiar, but it often asks for a sign-in, it does not record who uploaded what, and anyone with the link can delete or rearrange. Fine for ten close friends; messy for a hundred guests.

3. A group chat (asks: be in the group)

The default, and the worst archive. Chats compress photos, strip originals, bury them between messages, and miss everyone who was not added. The “album” belongs to whoever made the group, which is rarely the couple or the organiser.

4. A hashtag (asks: post publicly)

Hashtags only catch what people are willing to publish. Most of the best photos never get posted. They work as a complement, not as the collection.

What to look for if you go with a QR album

  • No app, no account for guests, on iPhone and Android. If a guest has to download something, you are back to option 3.
  • A name on every upload, so you know who to thank and who to ask for the original.
  • Host controls: hide or delete a photo, remove a guest, pause new joins when the night is over.
  • Open or private: a live shared gallery for a party, a blind-upload reveal for a wedding.
  • A clean exit: one-tap save to the phone or a ZIP for the laptop, with a clear storage window and a reminder before it ends.
  • One-time pricing per event, not a subscription you forget to cancel.

Making it actually happen on the night

Technology is the easy part. Three things move the upload rate more than anything else: a card on every table (not one sign at the door), one spoken sentence from the host or the DJ, and a live photo wall on the venue screen so guests see their shots appear and want to add more. On ShareCam the wall is included with the Unlimited package and opens in any browser at sharecam.app/wall.
