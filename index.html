How it works.

  1. First

    Create your event.

    Name, date, open or private. Your QR code is ready in about a minute, and you don’t need an account to start.

  2. Then

    Put it on the tables.

    Choose from 16 card designs with your QR code built in. Print four to an A4 page with cut marks, or send the invite link.

  3. After

    Watch it fill up.

    Photos and videos from every phone land in one live gallery. Afterwards, save them all to Photos or download a ZIP.

Nobody downloads anything.

How it’s handled

  • Off until the host turns it on and accepts the host terms.
  • Each guest decides for themselves. Declining costs nothing and never limits access to the album.
  • Selfies are processed on our servers (Amazon Web Services, Frankfurt), not on the phone. The search signature is deleted as soon as the search finishes, within 24 hours at the latest, and the selfie is never added to the album.
  • There’s no list of people. Even the host searches with their own selfie.
  • Never sold, never used to train AI, never used to identify anyone across events.
  • Not offered in Illinois, Texas or Washington, in China, Russia or Vietnam, or anywhere we can’t confirm the location.

Questions

Anything else, write to app.sharecam@gmail.com.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. The QR code opens in the phone’s browser. Guests type their name and upload. Anyone who prefers the app can use it, and everything lands in the same album.

Do guests need an iPhone?

No. Guests can join from any phone with a browser, Android included. You can host from the web or the iPhone app (iOS 16.4 or later).

Is it a subscription?

No. Packages are one-time purchases for one event, and nothing renews. Every event starts on the free Spark package.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, any time from the app or the web dashboard. Upgrading charges the full price of the new package. The event name and date lock once you choose a paid package, so check them first.

What happens if the album fills up?

New uploads stop, and guests see a message asking the host to upgrade. You can upgrade from the app at any time.

How long are photos kept?

From 7 days (Spark) to 1 year (Unlimited), counted from your event date. The app sends a notification before storage ends (2 days before on Spark, 5 on Mini and Party, 14 on Wedding and Unlimited), so keep notifications on. You can save everything to Photos or download a ZIP at any time.

Are photos saved in full resolution?

Photos are resized on the phone to 2,048 px on the long edge before upload. That keeps uploads quick on venue Wi-Fi, but they’re not the camera originals.

Can guests see each other’s photos?

Only if you want them to. An open album is shared live. In private mode, only you see everything until you reveal it, either by hand or on a schedule.

Can I remove a photo or a guest?

Yes. Hosts can hide or delete any photo, remove a guest and pause new joins. Guests can report a photo, and it goes straight to the host.

Is face matching available where I live?

It isn’t offered in Illinois, Texas or Washington, in China, Russia or Vietnam, and it stays off anywhere we can’t confirm the location. Where it is available, it’s an optional $9.99 add-on per event, and every guest chooses for themselves.

How do refunds work?

Purchases in the app go through Apple, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com. Purchases on the web are refunded by us through Paddle, under our refund policy. If something went wrong on our side, write to us first.

Who processes web payments?

Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle runs the checkout, sends the receipt and invoice and handles tax; we never see your card details. See the web purchase terms. Purchases in the iPhone app are processed by Apple.

Can’t find your events on the web?

Sign in on the web with the same account you use in the app (Settings → Account). If you never signed in there, do that in the app first. Signed in with Apple in the app? Web sign-in with Apple is coming.

Can I use it for birthdays and other parties?

Yes. Spark is free for up to 10 guests, and the smaller packages are made for dinners, birthdays and parties.
